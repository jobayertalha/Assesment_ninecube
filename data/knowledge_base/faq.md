# Frequently asked questions

## How do I contact customer support?

Email support@smartshop.example or call 09610000000. The team is available Saturday to Thursday, 9:00 AM–6:00 PM Bangladesh time, and is closed on Fridays.

## How much is delivery outside Dhaka?

Delivery outside Dhaka costs BDT 120. It usually takes around 3–5 business days, although courier or holiday delays can affect the estimate.

## Can I return an order?

You can ask for a return within 7 calendar days of delivery if the product is unopened or arrived defective. Contact customer support to have the request reviewed. A request is not automatically approved.

## Do you deliver outside Bangladesh?

No. SmartShop BD currently delivers within Bangladesh only.

## Where can I check product details?

The product catalogue lists the specifications available to us. If a detail is not listed there, contact support before placing an order rather than assuming the product has that feature.
