# About SmartShop BD

SmartShop BD is an online electronics shop serving customers across Bangladesh. Customers can browse the catalogue and place orders through the website. Product availability can change, so the product page is the best place to check whether an item is currently in stock.

## Getting help

For questions about an order, delivery or a product, customers can email **support@smartshop.example** or call **09610000000**.

The support team is available Saturday through Thursday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Bangladesh time. The team is closed on Fridays.

## Before placing an order

Please check the product page for the latest availability and listed specifications. The information in this knowledge base does not cover every checkout or payment-provider detail; support can help with questions that are not answered here.
