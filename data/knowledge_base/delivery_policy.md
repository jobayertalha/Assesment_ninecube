# Delivery information

SmartShop BD delivers within Bangladesh. At present, international delivery is not available.

## Charges and estimated delivery times

| Delivery area | Charge | Estimated time |
|---|---:|---|
| Within Dhaka | BDT 60 | 1–2 business days |
| Outside Dhaka | BDT 120 | 3–5 business days |

Delivery times are estimates rather than guaranteed dates. Heavy rain, public holidays and courier delays can affect when an order arrives. If an order seems delayed, customers can contact support with their order details.
