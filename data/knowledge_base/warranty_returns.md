# Warranty and returns

This page summarizes the warranty periods and the return process used in the SmartShop BD sample knowledge base. The manufacturer’s full warranty terms should be checked before purchase.

## Warranty periods

| Product | Manufacturer warranty |
|---|---|
| Nova A1 Smartphone | 12 months |
| Nova B2 Smartphone | 12 months |
| EchoBuds Lite | 6 months |

The warranty does not cover physical damage caused by misuse. For a specific claim, support may need to review the issue and the applicable manufacturer terms.

## Requesting a return

Customers may contact support within **7 calendar days of delivery** if an item is unopened or arrived defective. To help the team review the request, email **support@smartshop.example** with the order number and a short description of the problem.

A return request is reviewed by customer support; it is not automatically approved. This policy summary does not specify refund processing times or any restocking fee, so those details should be confirmed with support rather than assumed.
